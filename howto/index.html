Operating manual FS-2

How to use the Tuner

A tuner built like a vintage drum machine, the Metronome FS-1’s companion. It listens through your microphone and shows the note you play and how far it is from being in tune, for guitar, bass, violin or any instrument.

01 Quick start

  1. Switch it on with On / Off (or Space) and allow the microphone when your browser asks.
  2. Pick a tuning with the keys at the bottom: guitar, bass, violin or chromatic.
  3. Play one string and let it ring.
  4. Tune until the needle stands upright in the green zone and the big LED glows bright green.

02 The display

← Swipe to see the whole display →

  1. The note nearest to what you play, in red LED segments; a dash without a note.
  2. Sharp and octave: ♯ lights for sharp notes (A♯), and the octave number follows (A4 is the A above middle C).
  3. The scale from −50 to +50 cents (a cent is a hundredth of a semitone): green in the middle, yellow, orange and red further out.
  4. The needle: left of centre you’re flat (tune up), right of it sharp (tune down). Without a note it rests past −50.
  5. Strings of the selected tuning; the one you’re nearest to lights up. “Chromatic” in chromatic mode.
  6. Frequency of the note you play, in hertz.

The display dims while the tuner is switched off.

03 Keys & knobs

Knobs: drag up or down (hold Shift for fine steps), scroll, or use the arrow keys when selected. Double-click a knob to reset it.

On / Off

Switches the microphone, and the tuner, on and off.

Input

Microphone gain, −12 to +24 dB (double-click: 0 dB), with LEDs showing the input level. Turn it up for quiet instruments.

440
Ref. A4

The reference pitch, 420–460 Hz. Click and type a value, drag or scroll (Shift for fine steps), double-click for 440 Hz.

Tone

The tuning fork: plays a steady A at the reference pitch to tune to by ear. Press again to stop.

Volume

How loud the tone plays (double-click: 50 %).

Tune LED

Bright green when you’re in tune, through yellow to red (and dimmer) the further off you are. A faint glow means it’s listening but hears no note.

06 Questions

The needle doesn’t move

Check that On / Off is lit and the input LEDs move when you play. If they barely move, turn Input up. Play one string at a time and let it ring.

The browser doesn’t ask for the microphone, or it was refused

Allow the microphone for this site in your browser’s settings (the icon left of the address) and switch on again.

Why would I change the reference pitch?

To match an orchestra or a recording that isn’t at 440 Hz: many orchestras tune to 442 Hz, baroque ensembles often lower. Everything the tuner shows follows the reference.

Can I use it offline, like an app?

Yes. Install it from your browser (“Add to Home Screen” on iPhone and iPad, “Install app” in Chrome and Edge). It then works without a connection and updates itself when you’re online. Your settings are remembered.
